Personal use · November 2026

Terms / use information

This page explains the current scope and limits of an owner-operated personal application. It does not create a subscription or a public software-distribution offer.

Purpose

Senior Advertiser Finder supports research into businesses appearing in public advertising sources, an ordered market queue, and local preparation of outreach drafts. The owner controls the selected markets, sources, Google connection and Start/Pause setting.

Human review

Automated research accuracy is still being validated. Models may misread a name, confuse nearby content or mistake editorial material for an advertisement. Saved evidence, source pages, business details and every draft need review before they inform an outreach decision. A generated record or count is not a guarantee of accuracy or business suitability.

Current limits

Email sending, text sending and Salesforce CSV export are disabled. The app prepares drafts for review and does not conduct outreach. Public-source availability, local processing speed, Google authorization and network conditions affect operation. The computer must be running for its background work to continue.

Connected services and source material

Google access is optional and applies to the selected queue spreadsheet. Google and public publication or search services have their own terms and access conditions. Publicly accessible material can still be subject to copyright or source restrictions. The app’s evidence links support review of the original material.

Local control

Pause research from the dashboard, review connection status, and revoke Google access through the owner’s Google Account when it is no longer wanted. Closing the dashboard alone does not stop the background app. The owner manages the local data folder and selected Google Sheet.
