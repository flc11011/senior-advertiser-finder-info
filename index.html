Personal Windows application

Public advertising research.
Evidence and drafts to review.

Senior Advertiser Finder helps its owner research public publications, organize potential business advertisers, and prepare editable outreach drafts for review.

An owner-operated local application with an optional Google Sheets queue connection.

01

Organize markets

Set a location, senior center, advertising price and lead target. The app tracks counts and research status in an ordered queue.

02

Review evidence

Inspect a business’s saved advertising evidence and original source page. Local OCR and an Ollama vision model assist research; accuracy is still being validated.

03

Prepare drafts

Edit and save email and text drafts on the Windows computer. Email sending, text sending and Salesforce CSV export are disabled.

Optional Google connection

One selected queue spreadsheet

Google sign-in lets the app read and update the queue spreadsheet selected by its owner, using the drive.file permission. Queue inputs, counts and status synchronize with that Sheet. Leads, advertising evidence and outreach drafts stay in the local app database.
